About

I'm in my final year of a BA at McGill, majoring in political science with minors in history and economics. My work sits where health policy meets technology and international affairs.

I'm currently a Corporate Strategy Intern at Dialogue Health Technologies and a Junior Research Fellow with the NATO Association of Canada's Global Health and Security Program. Before that, I researched Canadian healthcare policy at SecondStreet.org and was one of six students across Canada selected as a Summer Fellow at the Institute for Liberal Studies.

Outside of work and school, I lead partnerships and outreach for student organizations and volunteer at a women's shelter in Montreal.
